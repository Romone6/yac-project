# Pathway to Entry

**Pathway to Entry** is a regional post-school pathway platform built to reduce the information gap between choosing a future direction and successfully reaching university, TAFE, training, defence, emergency-services, or skilled-employment entry.

Live project: https://pathwaytoentry.com

## Current reach

- implemented across **40+ schools**
- informed by a structured consultation involving **200 respondents**
- consolidates **103 funding opportunities**
- tracks **59 major application and pathway dates**
- has received endorsement from **state and federal education ministers**
- has supported pathway programming involving the **NSW Police Force** and **Army and Navy recruitment personnel**

## Problem

Students are often given broad career advice but still have to discover critical deadlines, application processes, scholarships, entry schemes, required documents, and alternative pathways across disconnected systems.

Regional students face an additional coordination problem: the information exists, but it is fragmented across institutions and is often discovered too late.

Pathway to Entry is designed as infrastructure for that gap.

## Product direction

The platform brings together:

- university and tertiary pathways
- TAFE and vocational options
- employment and apprenticeship pathways
- funding and scholarship opportunities
- critical application dates
- entry requirements and application steps
- alternative and non-linear pathways
- practical tools for turning a chosen pathway into completed actions

## Research and consultation

The project combines product development with regional youth consultation. Evidence from the consultation is used to identify where students disengage, which information is missing, and which interventions are likely to reduce avoidable drop-off.

The objective is not simply to publish more information. It is to make the transition from **“I know what I want to do”** to **“I successfully entered that pathway”** easier to execute.

## Engineering

This repository contains the web application and supporting product implementation for Pathway to Entry.

The project is developed as a production service rather than a static information page, with the longer-term goal of creating proactive pathway tooling that can adapt to deadlines, eligibility, funding, and individual student decisions.
